## What this company sells

Physical materials, not software. Mitra Chem's commercial product is iron-based cathode
active material — lithium iron phosphate (LFP) and lithium manganese iron phosphate (LMFP) —
manufactured for Western battery cell makers and energy-storage OEMs as a non-China supply
source. It operates The Foundry, an instrumented shared pilot manufacturing facility covering
wet chemistry, hydrometallurgy, particle sizing, drying and heat treatment.

Mitra Chem markets a full-stack AI platform of physics-based models, digital twins and
engineering co-pilots. That platform is INTERNAL tooling applied to Mitra Chem's own data,
codebases and manufacturing operations. It is not sold as a software product and exposes no
external developer access. Do not infer an API from it.
